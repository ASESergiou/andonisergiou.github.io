  <div class="home-text">

    <p>
      I am a cognitive anthropologist studying the evolution and computational
      foundations of symbolic cognition and culture.
    </p>

    <p>
      My research asks how humans became capable of constructing, transmitting,
      and transforming meanings that cannot be learned through direct observation
      alone. I am particularly interested in how symbolic relationships allow
      humans to move beyond immediately experienced states of the world and
      collectively build new ones.
    </p>

    <p>
      I bring together cultural evolution, cognitive science, anthropology,
      semiotics, and computational modelling to investigate the emergence and
      evolution of symbolic representation.
    </p>
